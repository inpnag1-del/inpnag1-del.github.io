Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Srinidhi Automation respects your privacy. If you would like us to delete the information we hold about you, please follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to inpnag@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. In the email, include the WhatsApp phone number you used to contact us.
  3. We may ask you to confirm that the number belongs to you.

What we delete

Once your request is verified, we delete your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, message history and any order details we stored about you, except records we are legally required to keep (for example, tax or invoice records).

How long it takes

We complete deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email when it is done.
